Get started with Rocket league

Welcome to your Rocket League journey! This free-to-play game is available on multiple platforms, and getting started is easy. Follow these steps to jump into the action.

  1. Visit Epic Games Store.
  2. Click 'Get' to download the game for free.
  3. Install and launch Rocket League to create your account.
